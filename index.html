Project Details

Case Studies

A closer look at the business problems, Salesforce solutions and skills demonstrated in each project.

Mzansi GreenTech Solutions

+

Business Problem

The organisation required better CRM configuration, user access control, consistent data and clearer separation between Sales and Operations.

Salesforce Solution

The solution included company configuration, role hierarchy, profiles, permissions, opportunity fields and a custom Project object linked to Opportunities.

Security

Designed department-based access for Sales and Operations while using the role hierarchy to provide appropriate management visibility.

Automation & Validation

Validation requirements included preventing past installation start dates and requiring an assigned technician before permit approval.

HealthConnect Appointment Manager

+

Business Problem

Paper-based and basic calendar processes created risks around double booking, missed appointments, fragmented data and limited reporting.

Data Model

The proposed model separated Patients, Doctors, Appointments and Departments while maintaining relationships between the records.

Security

Access requirements differentiated between doctors, nurses, receptionists, department heads and leadership.

Automation

Requirements included appointment conflict prevention, availability checks, confirmation emails and appointment reminders.

CloudSphere Technical Support

+

Business Problem

CloudSphere was experiencing increased technical support demand, slow responses, performance issues and customer dissatisfaction.

Service Cloud

The proposed solution uses Cases, Record Types, Service Console, Omni-Channel and a structured support process.

Three-Tier Support

The requirements defined Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 support with different escalation timeframes measured using business hours.

Communication & Reporting

The solution included Email-to-Case, auto-response, professional email templates, reports and dashboards for support visibility.

Shipwrights Ltd.

+

Business Problem

Shipwrights required a scalable Salesforce approach to support international sales, customer management, products, pricing, reporting and fulfilment.

International Sales

The requirements included multi-currency, international customers, product and service catalogues and flexible pricing.

Sales Process

The proposed process covers prospects, opportunities, trade-show leads, referrals, campaigns, partners and online sources.

Reporting & Scalability

The solution focuses on pipeline visibility, international revenue, closed sales, service delivery and future scalability.
